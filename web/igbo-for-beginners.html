Igbo for Beginners: First Conversational Phrases

Welcome to your first steps in Igbo. Learn how to greet family, ask for someone's name, and express gratitude in authentic Asụsụ Igbo.

1. Greeting and Introducing Yourself

• Kedụ aha gị? — What is your name?
• Aha m bụ... — My name is...
• Nnọọ — Welcome
• Daalụ rinne — Thank you very much

2. Essential Food & Market Words

• Ji: Yam
• Mmiri: Water
• Ọnụahịa: Price / Cost
• Ego: Money
