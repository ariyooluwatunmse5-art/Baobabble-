Learn Yoruba: From First Greetings to Fluent Oratory

Spoken by over 45 million people across Nigeria, Benin, and the global African diaspora, Yoruba is one of the most expressive, musical, and philosophically rich languages in human civilization.

1. The Science of the 3 Tone Registers

Yoruba is a strict tonal language. The pitch contour of each vowel dictates the lexical meaning of the word:

1. High Tone (Á): Acute accent (´) — Pronounced at the high musical note Mí.

2. Mid Tone (A): No mark — Pronounced at the comfortable conversational baseline Re.

3. Low Tone (À): Grave accent (`) — Pronounced with a deep chest resonance at Dò.

The Classic Minimal Triplet:
• Ọkọ (Mid-Mid): Husband
• Ọkọ̀ (Low-Low): Car / Vehicle / Canoe
• Ọkọ́ (High-High): Hoe / Agricultural tool

2. Respect Culture: The Honorific "Ẹ"

In Yoruba, greeting elders or respected figures with the singular "O" is considered taboo. You always use the honorific plural Ẹ:

  • Báwo ni? — Casual "How are you?" (Used only for peers and younger friends).
  • Ẹ n lẹ o — Polite general greeting ("Greetings to you").
  • Ẹ kú àárọ̀ — Respectful morning greeting to an elder or parent.
  • Ẹ ṣe púpọ̀ — "Thank you very much" (respectful).

3. The Baobabble CEFR Course Path

Level A1: Seeds of Courtesy

Master phonetics, alphabet, tones, family titles, morning courtesies, and introducing yourself.

Level A2: Daily Life & Balogun Market

Shopping, bargaining (dín owó kù), dining (isu, jollof, amala), and directions around Lagos and Ibadan.

Level B1: Kinship & Narrative

Complex verb serializations (fi... hàn), storytelling, past actions, and visiting elder compounds.

Level B2: Social Ceremonies & Music

Traditional weddings (Ìgbéyàwó), naming ceremonies (Ìsọmọlórúkọ), and Highlife/Afrobeats lyrics.

Level C1: Philosophy & Oríkì Poetry

Deciphering ancestral praise poetry (Oríkì), ethical treaties, and historical leadership speeches.

Level C2: Proverbial Fluency (Òwe)

"Òwe lẹṣin ọ̀rọ̀" (Proverbs are the horses of speech). Debate with the nuance of an Odù Ifá scholar.
