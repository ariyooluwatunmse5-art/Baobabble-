Hausa for Beginners: Essential Phrases for West African Travel

Whether traveling to Abuja, Kano, Zaria, or Niamey, mastering these core Hausa courtesies will open doors of genuine hospitality.

1. Greeting in Any Setting

• Sannu: Universal friendly greeting (Hello)
• Ina kwana?: Good morning (Literally: How did you sleep?)
• Lafiya lau: Peace / Very well (Universal standard reply)
• Na gode: Thank you

2. Market Bargaining Basics

• Nawa ne?: How much is this?
• Yana da tsada: It is too expensive
• Rage min mana: Please reduce the price for me
