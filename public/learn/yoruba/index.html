Tones: The Heart of Yoruba

Tones distinguish words completely. For example:
• Ọkọ̀ (Low-Low) = Vehicle or Car
• Ọkọ (Mid-Mid) = Husband
• Ọkọ́ (Mid-High) = Hoe / Farm Tool
