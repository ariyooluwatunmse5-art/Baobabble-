Frequently Asked Questions

Which African languages are offered?

Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, Amharic, Swahili, isiZulu, and Lingala, spanning West, East, Central, and Southern Africa.

How does the tone engine help me?

It breaks down high, mid, and low tones into musical solfège (Dò-Re-Mí) and teaches minimal pairs so you never confuse words like husband, vehicle, and hoe.
