Yoruba for Beginners: Your First 5 Days of Learning

Starting your journey with Èdè Yorùbá? Here are the 5 foundational principles to build confidence in conversation immediately.

Rule 1: Never Skip the Honorific 'Ẹ'

When speaking to anyone older than you or in positions of respect, use Ẹ kú àárọ̀ instead of casual greetings. Respect is the foundational pillar of Yoruba culture.

Rule 2: Master Vowels with Underdots (Ẹ and Ọ)

• E: Sounds like 'ay' in 'say'.
• Ẹ (with subdot): Sounds like 'eh' in 'bed'.
• O: Sounds like 'oh' in 'go'.
• Ọ (with subdot): Sounds like 'aw' in 'law'.

Rule 3: Count from 1 to 5

1 = Ení / Oókan, 2 = Èjì / Méjì, 3 = Ẹ̀ta / Mẹ́ta, 4 = Ẹ̀rin / Mẹ́rin, 5 = Àrún / Mẹ́rùn.
