🇬🇭 ASANTE TWI COURSE SYLLABUS

Learn Twi: The Gold Coast Language of Royal Eloquence

Spoken by over 10 million people in Ghana and West Africa, Asante Twi is famous for its warm hospitality (Akwaaba), philosophical day names, and proverb-laden oratory.

1. The Akan Soul Day Names (Kradin)

Every Akan child receives a soul name based on the day of the week they were born:

  • • Monday: Kojo (M) / Adwoa (F)
  • • Tuesday: Kwabena (M) / Abena (F)
  • • Wednesday: Kwaku (M) / Akua (F)
  • • Thursday: Yaw (M) / Yaa (F)
  • • Friday: Kofi (M) / Afia (F)
  • • Saturday: Kwame (M) / Ama (F)
  • • Sunday: Kwasi (M) / Akosua (F)

2. Essential Asante Courtesies

  • Akwaaba — Welcome
  • Ɛte sɛn? — How are you?
  • Ɛyɛ — It is good / Fine
  • Me daase — Thank you
  • Nante yiye — Walk well (Goodbye)
