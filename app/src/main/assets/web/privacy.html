Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Effective Worldwide

1. Our Core Commitment to Privacy

At Baobabble ("we", "our", or "us"), we believe that learning your ancestral heritage or acquiring a new language should never come at the cost of your digital privacy. We do not sell your personal data or broker speech audio to third-party ad networks.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information: If you create a profile, we store your chosen username, email address, selected learning languages, CEFR level, XP, and streak milestones.

Learning Progress & Quizzes: We track completed lessons, quiz accuracy, review timestamps, and milestone unlocks to personalize your spaced repetition review.

Voice Audio: In speaking and pronunciation exercises, audio is processed locally on your device via standard Android/Web speech APIs or transmitted ephemerally to the AI tutor without being stored permanently.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Baobabble welcomes learners of all ages, including children connecting with family heritage. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.

4. Contact Our Data Protection Officer

If you have questions regarding data retention or wish to exercise your rights under GDPR/CCPA, please contact: privacy@baobabble.app.
