OUR ROOTS & PURPOSE

Rooting Humanity in the Languages of the African Continent

The Living Baobab Tree

In traditional African folklore, the ancient Baobab tree (Adansonia) is the Tree of Life. Under its sprawling canopy, village elders gathered to share proverbs, tell historical epics, settle community disputes, and teach the youth their ancestral heritage.

Baobabble was founded to bring that sacred digital canopy to millions of heritage speakers, diaspora families, travelers, and language enthusiasts worldwide.

Why Accuracy Matters: Eliminating AI Hallucination

Mainstream language apps have historically marginalized African languages, treating them as low-resource afterthoughts or populating them with crude machine translations that strip away tones and cultural nuances.

At Baobabble, every language is governed by an authoritative Linguistic Profile:

  • Tonal Pitch Science: Precise High (Mí), Mid (Re), and Low (Dò) pitch acoustics.
  • Respect & Honorifics: Teaching honorific plurals (such as Yoruba Ẹ) and relational titles.
  • Philosophical Depth: Integrating Ubuntu, Yoruba Àṣà, and Akan soul days into conversational lessons.

Worldwide Team

Our linguists, native-speaking educators, and engineers are based across Lagos, Nairobi, Johannesburg, Accra, London, and San Francisco.

Pedagogical Standards

Courses adhere strictly to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR) levels A1 through C2.
