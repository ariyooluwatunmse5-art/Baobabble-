🇳🇬 🇳🇪 HARSHEN HAUSA SYLLABUS

Learn Hausa: The Commercial Lingua Franca of the Sahel

Spoken by over 80 million people from Kano to Niamey, Hausa is an Afroasiatic language renowned for its ancient trans-Saharan trading heritage and poetic eloquence.

1. The Hook Letters (Glottalized & Ejective Consonants)

Standard Boko Hausa orthography features three distinctive hooked letters created by glottal closure:

  • Ɓ / ɓ: Implosive B — Formed by drawing air inward slightly into the larynx.
  • Ɗ / ɗ: Implosive D — Tongue tip on alveolar ridge with glottal suction.
  • Ƙ / ƙ: Ejective K — Sharp, explosive pop of air behind the velum.

2. Universal Everyday Courtesies

  • Sannu da zuwa — Welcome
  • Ina kwana? — How did you sleep? (Morning greeting)
  • Lafiya lau — In good health / Very well
  • Na gode — Thank you
  • Sai an jima — See you later
